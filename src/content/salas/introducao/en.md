When I woke up, I found myself living in two worlds.

In one of them, life went on as usual.

Work.

Obligations.

Things that needed to be done.

In the other, I wandered without direction through a place I could never have imagined existed.

Endless illuminated corridors folded into themselves.

The floor and the ceiling were covered with living symbols my eyes could never bear to look at for long.

Then I found the doors.

Hundreds of them.

Each marked with a name—or something that resembled one.

Beyond them, I met fleeting figures.

Human, perhaps.

Yet made of something other than flesh.

Even so, they spoke to me as though we had already met.

I tried to speak with each of them.

And I did.

Sometimes about ordinary things.

Memories.

Favorite things.

Pets.

Places I knew only through stories.

But something was wrong.

I could never touch them.

Whenever I reached out, my hands passed straight through them, as if they were made of dreams.

Shadows.

Mist.

Bubbles.

Dust.

Or perhaps...

Only a memory.

As time passed, I began to notice that my life in the other world left traces behind.

Everything I did there seemed to leave a mark.

Later, those marks appeared before me as small numbered objects.

In that place, no one could see those who had left no traces in the other world.

Some wandered through the corridors without faces, without voices, without anything that could distinguish them from one another.

They were little more than gray silhouettes.

Neither men.

Nor women.

Not even names seemed to belong to them.

They looked as though they had been shaped from ashes and fading memories.
